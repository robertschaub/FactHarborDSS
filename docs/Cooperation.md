# Partnerships and funded development

FactHarborDSS needs practical cases, independent judgment, readers, engineering capacity and resources to test whether the intended product is useful. Two cooperation routes take priority: **practical cooperation around real needs** and **a funded development phase with defined deliverables**. They can reinforce each other.

Existing Alpha reports and DSS planning drafts provide a starting point. DSS software, comparative results and qualification remain to be developed. The current sequence begins with public-web claim checking; the file-based decision pilot follows later.

## Practical cooperation

### Objective

Help a reader inspect a sufficiently supported claim assessment, see conflicting evidence and recognize gaps. Later, test whether one bounded decision workflow helps a partner compare alternatives against agreed goals and constraints.

### What a partner could contribute

For the initial web phase, useful contributions are precise public factual claims, source leads, independent evidence judgments and readers willing to try the workflow. A decision practitioner or private file collection is not a prerequisite for that phase.

For the later FileEvidence pilot, a partner would contribute representative cases from one recurring decision family, a permitted versioned evidence collection, relevant goals and constraints, and readers to evaluate the results. The family and intended use still need to be selected.

### What needs to be developed and tested

The initial phase needs a working capture/replay path, inspectable development assessments and a reader check. A subsequent comparison must test assessment quality on fixed evidence. The later file pilot additionally needs retrieval and decision-comparison evaluation across representative cases.

Together, these stages should examine traceability, evidence gaps, justified conclusions, useful comparisons, reader effort, time and cost per case. Those are evaluation questions, not promised improvements.

Agree scope, success criteria, data handling, permitted processing, recipients and responsibilities before the relevant work starts. Keep protected evaluation cases and answers out of public issues.

## A funded development phase

### Objective

Provide capacity for a bounded phase with explicit objectives, deliverables and continuation criteria. The immediate candidate is web evidence acquisition, PostgreSQL capture/replay and minimal development claim assessment, followed by a separately scoped comparative evaluation.

### What is available

The FactHarbor application (Alpha) offers an existing report experience to learn from. DSS has planning drafts, architecture, worked examples and authored acceptance scenarios. The FactHarbor association provides the non-profit setting. These foundations require review and refinement; they are not a finished implementation specification or qualified product.

The [project concept](Concept.md) and [planned architecture](Architecture.md) are documented in this repository. Any agreed contribution needs the relevant self-contained scope and acceptance conditions before dependent work.

### Contribution sought

Funding and engineering capacity for development and evaluation, ideally combined with appropriate claim examples, independent judgments and readers. Operational decision cases and permitted file collections become relevant to the later pilot.

### Deliverables to agree

A bounded agreement could cover reviewed foundations and resolved scope choices, the implemented milestone, focused verification results, a reader evaluation and a report on quality, usefulness, time, cost and limits. Comparative claim evaluation and the later decision pilot require their own defined scope and resources.

Set scope, duration, budget, responsibilities and review arrangements together. There is no DSS donation programme, established funding target, committed partner, delivery date or guaranteed product outcome. A successful development check does not automatically qualify results for operational use.

## Business development and communication

Other useful contributions include partner introductions, research collaboration, fundraising advice and applications, marketing, presentations and clear public communication. These support the practical and funded routes rather than constitute a product-validation claim.

## Agree a concrete first step

Describe the need or contribution, who would use the result, what evidence would show it helps, and the resources or expertise available. Then agree a bounded next step.

For a conversation, use [the public contact routes](Links.md#start-a-conversation). For a public technical proposal, use a [repository issue](https://github.com/robertschaub/FactHarborDSS/issues) when you have access. Do not include private evidence or protected evaluation material in the initial message.

Cooperation and sponsorship do not themselves grant publication, deployment, processing or operational-reliance permission. Repository-wide licensing, website hosting and any future application release remain separate decisions. For scoped repository work, see the [contribution guide](https://github.com/robertschaub/FactHarborDSS/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).
