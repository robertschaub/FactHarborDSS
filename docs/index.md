## Evidence for better-informed decisions

FactHarborDSS is a planned tool for **checking factual claims** and, later, **comparing courses of action**. It aims to make conclusions easier to inspect and challenge—with clear evidence, reasons and uncertainty.

Designed for people and, later, working AI agents.

## Building on existing work

The project builds on lessons from the [FactHarbor application (Alpha)](FactHarbor.md) and [Evidence-Gated Agents](Charter-and-EGA.md): examine what supports a conclusion, and keep permission to share or act separate.

It is developed within the [FactHarbor association](Background.md), a Swiss non-profit.

## Where we are now

**Planning stage — 28 October 2026.** The first development phase would check public-web claims and preserve the evidence examined. Implementation has not started; DSS benefits have yet to be demonstrated.

Detailed requirements, specifications and architecture documents currently live in the [related private repository](Architecture.md#planning-documents).
