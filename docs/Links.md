## Start a conversation

Contact **Robert Schaub** through [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/robertschaub/) or [info@factharbor.ch](mailto:info@factharbor.ch), the contact address published on the [FactHarbor overview](https://robertschaub.github.io/FactHarbor/about-factharbor/index.html).

Describe the practical need, contribution or cooperation you have in mind. The [partnership and funding page](Cooperation.md) outlines the two main routes. Agree a suitable channel and permissions before sharing confidential material; keep protected evaluation cases and answer keys out of public issues.
