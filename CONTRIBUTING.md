# Cooperation and contributions

FactHarborDSS welcomes practical cooperation, funded development and contributions to its planning and future implementation.

Start with [Partnerships and funded development](docs/Cooperation.md). It explains the two main routes: cases and readers to evaluate real needs, and funding or engineering capacity for a bounded development phase. Business development, fundraising and communication support are also useful.

For a conversation, use the [public contact routes](docs/Links.md#start-a-conversation). Propose documentation, evaluation or engineering work through a [repository issue](https://github.com/robertschaub/FactHarborDSS/issues): describe the need, who benefits and how usefulness could be checked.

The project is in planning. Cooperation terms, implementation scope, data permissions, budgets and continuation criteria must be agreed for the relevant work. No improved result, delivery date, active partnership or funding commitment is implied.

Keep customer inputs, private evidence and reports, credentials, private paths, raw logs and protected evaluation cases or answers out of public contributions. Agree a suitable exchange route before sharing restricted material. Check reuse terms and preserve source notices; repository-wide licensing remains undecided.

Keep changes focused, distinguish planned capabilities from demonstrated results, and report checks actually run. Contributions must not depend on private planning documents. Use the [README's build instructions](README.md#preview-locally), check relative links and run `git diff --check`.

For editorial documentation changes, the checks above are sufficient; no development bet or evaluation panel is needed. Substantive requirements and architecture changes still need independent review. The [workflow](docs/agents/Workflow.md) covers development, analytical changes and authorized delegation. Agents also read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md).
